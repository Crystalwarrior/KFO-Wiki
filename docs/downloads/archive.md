## #14: The Loopening (Thursday, January 23rd, 2025)

INCLUDES:

* All KFO base content tracks, now properly LOOPED! This means songs will no longer fade out and fade in, and just loop seamlessly.
* Some automation was used for the loopening! Therefore, if you find a loop that does not sound right, please report it to me in #tech-support and I'll make a note of it to fix in the next update!
